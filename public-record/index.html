02 / CONTEMPORARY COVERAGE

What was reported in 2016.

The 2016 reporting described the initial charges. The felony charges were dropped before trial following the introduction of new evidence.

A November 16, 2016 Insurance Journal article summarized fraud and conspiracy charges. It described an investigation triggered by a Farmers Insurance claim involving a collectible Porsche, and attributed details of an alleged paper trail to investigators. The article describes the initial charges, not every subsequent court action.

For case documents, see the Sonoma County Superior Court records section.
