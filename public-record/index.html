02 / CONTEMPORARY COVERAGE

What was reported in 2016 and 2017.

The published coverage is a record of what was alleged and announced at the time. It should be read in its original context.

A November 16, 2016 Insurance Journal article summarized fraud and conspiracy charges. It described an investigation triggered by a Farmers Insurance claim involving a collectible Porsche, and attributed details of an alleged paper trail to investigators. Allegations described at the charging stage are not, by themselves, the final disposition of a case.

A July 29, 2017 Bay City News report, carried by SFGate, described sentencing following statements by California insurance officials. The article reported 150 days in jail, five years of probation, and a joint restitution order. Those assertions are part of the historical reporting, even though later accounts challenge aspects of the outcome.

03 / POINTS IN DISPUTE

The details worth checking.

Information supplied for this site challenges several conclusions readers might draw from the older coverage. The claims below are presented as disputes, not as certified court findings.

01 / DISPOSITION

What happened to the charges?

The supplied account maintains the felonies were dropped or reduced and that later relief changed the record's status. A docket and signed orders are necessary to confirm whether, when, and how.

02 / CUSTODY & PROBATION

What was imposed—and served?

The later account disputes serving the reported 150-day jail term and the description of a five-year probation sentence. An announced sentence and actual time served are different matters.

03 / RESTITUTION & PAYMENT

What was owed and paid?

The 2017 article reported restitution. The later account disputes an obligation and any payment. An order, any modifications, and proof of actual payment would address separate questions.

04 / KNOWLEDGE & CLAIM

Who submitted the claim?

The supplied account disputes knowledge or participation in a scheme, receipt of insurance proceeds, or filing a stolen-vehicle claim. Those points require a distinction between allegations, adjudicated findings, and any investigative records.

AN ADDITIONAL DETAIL1954 or 1955?

The later description identifies a 1954 Porsche, while both articles refer to a 1955 Porsche 356 Speedster. This site does not treat either description as proof of the vehicle's identity.

05 / PRIMARY RECORDS

Check the court record yourself.

The most direct way to assess the final outcome is to inspect the docket and relevant Sonoma County Superior Court orders rather than rely exclusively on historical coverage—or on this review.

CASE REFERENCE PROVIDED FOR THIS REVIEW*SCR-715528-1*Confirm the reference and access with the court clerk. No certified disposition is hosted on this site.
COURTSonoma County Superior CourtCriminal Division · Hall of Justice, 625 Administration Drive, Santa Rosa, CA 95403
How to request records ↗

The court states that criminal case information is not provided through its public website. On-site public access and document-request procedures are available. Requested documents may be restricted or unavailable depending on the case's status; the clerk can clarify what can be released.

What documents would answer the questions?

The register of actions or certified docket, sentencing orders, any subsequent orders affecting the charges or sentence, and applicable restitution documents would be the most useful starting points. Custody served and payments made may require separate records.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What can readers conclude?

Was Constantine Petros sentenced in 2017?

A July 2017 Bay City News report stated that a jail sentence, probation, and restitution had been ordered. This website has not independently obtained the signed sentencing order.

Does that report prove jail time was served or restitution was paid?

No. A reported sentence or obligation does not alone document time actually served or payments actually made. These require the relevant records.

Where can the final case status be verified?

Contact Sonoma County Superior Court about case reference SCR-715528-1, confirming the reference with the clerk, and request the docket and applicable orders.
