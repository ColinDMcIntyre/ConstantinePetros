The material below presents Petros's account and directs readers to the formal court resource for the underlying record.

The account provided

What Petros says happened

Petros says the situation began with someone else’s scheme.

According to Petros, during a casual conversation he mentioned that he had once sold a car similar to his own, one that had left the country decades earlier. He says that information was later used by Chris Hatton to obtain a duplicate title for a similar vehicle and construct an insurance claim.

Petros says he had no knowledge of the plan and gave no consent to it. He says he did not report a car stolen or receive insurance money, but was nonetheless drawn into the investigation that followed.

Petros also disputes portions of subsequent reporting concerning jail time, restitution, and the final status of the charges. Readers seeking the formal disposition should consult the certified court file rather than relying on this page alone.
