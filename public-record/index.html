02 / CONTEMPORARY COVERAGE

What was reported in 2016 and 2017.

Two contemporary articles document the initial charges and reported sentencing. Later case activity belongs in the Sonoma County Superior Court record.

A November 16, 2016 Insurance Journal article summarized fraud and conspiracy charges. It described an investigation triggered by a Farmers Insurance claim involving a collectible Porsche, and attributed details of an alleged paper trail to investigators. The article describes the initial charges, not every subsequent court action.

A July 29, 2017 Bay City News report, carried by SFGate, described sentencing following statements by California insurance officials. The article reported 150 days in jail, five years of probation, and a joint restitution order. For later orders or modifications, consult the Sonoma County case record.

03 / CASE OUTCOME

What happened after the headlines.

Petros's account differs from the impression left by the 2017 coverage. The sections below summarize his position and direct readers to the Sonoma County Superior Court records process for case SCR-715528-1.

01 / DISPOSITION

Charges and subsequent relief

Petros says the felony charges were dropped or reduced and that subsequent relief changed the case record. The register of actions and signed orders show the formal sequence of court decisions.

02 / CUSTODY & PROBATION

Custody and probation

Petros says he did not serve the reported 150-day jail term and disputes the probation history described in the news. Sentencing orders and actual custody records address different questions.

03 / RESTITUTION & PAYMENT

Restitution and payments

Petros disputes a restitution obligation and says he made no restitution payment. The court file can show restitution orders and changes; payment history is a separate record.

04 / KNOWLEDGE & CLAIM

The insurance claim

Petros denies filing a stolen-vehicle claim, receiving insurance proceeds, or knowingly participating in an insurance-fraud scheme. The 2016 report summarizes investigators' original allegations.

AN ADDITIONAL DETAIL1954 or 1955?

Petros identifies the car as a 1954 Porsche; the 2016 and 2017 reports describe a 1955 Porsche 356 Speedster. Compare the source reports.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What can readers conclude?

Was Constantine Petros sentenced in 2017?

Yes, a July 2017 Bay City News report described jail, probation, and restitution. For the applicable court orders and later modifications, consult the official case record.

Does that report prove jail time was served or restitution was paid?

No. A sentence reported in the news does not establish actual time served or the amount paid. Those questions depend on custody and payment records.

Where can the final case status be verified?

Use the Sonoma County Superior Court criminal-record instructions to request the docket and relevant orders, referencing SCR-715528-1 and confirming the case number with the clerk.
